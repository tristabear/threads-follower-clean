threads-bot-filter

Flag likely bot/spam followers on your own Threads account, then bulk block/report the ones you pick.

1. Connect your browser

Open threads.net in another tab, log in, open DevTools (F12) → Console, and paste this whole script, then press Enter:

This runs entirely in your browser tab. It never sees or sends your password/cookies — it just lets your tab report its own network traffic to this local tool, and later replay actions you approve. Right after you paste it, a small "relay" popup will open — allow popups for threads.net if your browser blocks it, and leave that popup open (it can be minimized).

2. Capture your followers

On the threads.net tab, go to your profile → Followers, and scroll all the way through the list. Then open a few profiles that look like they might be bots (this helps capture richer data).
